## Invariant

Observation validation has one owner, and `validateV5()` returns an immutable accepted snapshot rather than the caller's mutable project.

## Implementation

`graph/ir.ts` owns observation edge identity, unknown sources, duplicate edges, and self-reference. `graph/order.ts` owns cycle detection and canonical `graph-cycle` diagnostics. The contract validator keeps authored shape, ids, keyframes, triggers, and perspective validation, then delegates graph semantics to `buildGraphIR`. The recursive validator cycle walk and inverted self-reference check are deleted.

Accepted projects are recursively cloned and deep-frozen. `Engine.load()` compiles and installs that accepted snapshot, so mutations to the authored object after load cannot alter the live runtime.

## Evidence

The red leg failed five assertion-level tests: legal sibling references were rejected, real self-reference and cycles had different diagnostics, duplicate edge identity drifted, and accepted values aliasing the caller remained mutable. The first green attempt exposed two stale `observation-cycle` expectations in quality and integration; a second green attempt exposed strict cast errors in typecheck. Both were fixed explicitly, and the final checks passed without weakening the behavioral tests.
