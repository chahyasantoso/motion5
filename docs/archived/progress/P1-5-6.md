## Invariant

A patch is republished only when its content actually changed, and a settled batch is delivered to exactly the listeners that existed when it settled.

## Implementation

`PatchRegistry` compares `values` and `sourceRevisions` structurally through `equalValues`, compares diagnostics field by field, and snapshots node listeners into arrays before notification. Subscription changes take effect on the next batch.

## Evidence

The red leg failed on real assertions: nested records republished, diagnostic key order mattered, and live node-listener sets skipped or added listeners during notification. The implementation and formatting-head runs passed the behavioral suite.

## Known gaps

Per-flush allocation remains O(graph) rather than O(dirty), and publisher dependents/compose wrappers are still rebuilt per flush. Those remain separate P2 work.
