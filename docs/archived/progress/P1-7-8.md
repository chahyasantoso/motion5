## Invariant

A flush requested from inside a patch subscriber is coalesced into one pending set and drained through the injected scheduler, so it publishes with zero additional clock ticks, and `GraphRuntime.flush()`/`invalidate()` are the only reentrancy policy in the package.

## Implementation

`GraphRuntime` takes an optional `Scheduler` and calls `#scheduleDrain()` whenever seeds are deferred, whether they arrive during notification or survive a failed flush. `#scheduledDrain` collapses repeated invalidations into a single follow-up, and the scheduled job never supplies a tick, so batch identity advances while the clock's frame number does not. `ProjectRuntime` and `Engine` thread the engine's existing scheduler through; no new clock or ticker exists anywhere on the path.

`GraphPublisher` is no longer reachable from `GraphRuntime`, so the publisher's throw-on-reentrancy guard is an internal assertion rather than a second public policy a subscriber can reach.

## Evidence

The red leg was assertion-level: with no scheduler wiring, a subscriber-triggered invalidation stayed in `pendingSeeds` forever and `registry.get("caption/label")` remained `undefined` after `scheduler.flush()`. The green suite asserts the drain with zero clock ticks, single-follow-up coalescing, and that `runtime.tick` is unchanged by a scheduled drain.

## Review findings addressed in-branch

1. **Scheduler failure could strand deferred work permanently.** `#scheduleDrain()` set its guard before calling `schedule()` and never cleared it on a synchronous throw, so every later invalidation skipped scheduling. The call is now wrapped, the guard is cleared before reporting `scheduler-failure`, and `#pendingSeeds` is left intact so a later invalidation retries. Covered by a regression whose first `schedule()` throws.
2. **A vacuous clock-subscription assertion.** The spy had been installed after constructing `GraphRuntime`, which could only prove nothing subscribed later. It is installed before construction and asserts exactly one call.
3. **Unrelated coverage was deleted.** The slice had narrowed the fixture to a single motion, dropping the monotonic-tick-across-detach/reattach and attached-seeds/disposal regressions. Both are restored verbatim against the full `hero/arm` + `caption/label` + `~/cursor` fixture, and the P1-8 publisher-surface assertion now lives in its own case instead of riding inside I-13.

## Known gaps

`GraphRuntime` still exposes `registry` and `binding`; that is internal-only after P1-9 but remains a wide internal surface. The scheduler is optional on `GraphRuntimeOptions`, so a runtime constructed without one still queues seeds that only a later flush can drain: acceptable while `Engine` always injects one, and worth tightening if another caller appears.
