## Geometric & Sub-Mesh Contract

### `warm-batch-jar.glb`

Binary glTF 2.0 (`+Y` up, centered on X/Z, base resting at `y = 0`).

- **`JarGlass`**: Hollow lathe-turned apothecary glass jar body with thick base (`0.08`), outer radius `0.60`, beveled shoulder (`y = 1.05..1.22`), neck (`r = 0.44`), and flared rim lip (`y = 1.30..1.36`). World bounds: `[-0.600, 0.000, -0.600]` to `[0.600, 1.360, 0.600]`.
- **`JarGasket`**: Warm cream silicone seal ring (`#EFE6DA`) seated at `y = 1.24`, outer radius `0.43`. World bounds: `[-0.430, 1.222, -0.430]` to `[0.430, 1.258, 0.430]`.
- **`JarLid`**: Turned warm oak wooden lid with beveled cap (`r = 0.49`, cap height `0.14`), inner plug (`y = -0.06..0.00`), and top grip knob (`r = 0.14`, top at local `y = 0.278`), textured with embedded procedural 256×256 oak wood-grain PNG (`WarmOakWoodGrain`). Node translation is `[0, 1.32, 0]` with mesh vertices authored around local `(0, 0, 0)`. Local bounds: `[-0.490, -0.060, -0.490]` to `[0.490, 0.278, 0.490]`; world bounds at rest: `[-0.490, 1.260, -0.490]` to `[0.490, 1.598, 0.490]`.

### `warm-batch-cookie.glb`

Binary glTF 2.0 (centered at `(0, 0, 0)`, flat on XZ plane, decorated crown facing `+Y`, radius `0.42`, thickness `0.16`).

- **`CookieDough`**: Organic subdivided bakery cookie disc with domed bumpy crown, scalloped crinkled rim, smooth vertex normals, and embedded procedural 256×256 baked golden-brown crackle dough PNG (`WarmBakedCrackleDough`). World bounds: `[-0.420, -0.080, -0.420]` to `[0.420, 0.080, 0.420]`.
- **`ChocChunks`**: 22 irregular beveled dark-cocoa chunks (`#2E1810`, roughness `0.28`) embedded across the top dome and rim.
- **`SaltFlakes`**: 13 flat off-white translucent crystal flakes (`#FAF6F0`) scattered across the top crown.
