FROM GROVE TO CAN
STAGE 01 · DAWN HARVEST

Picked only after the November frost

Cold mountain air thickens the peel and locks aromatic oils inside every golden yuzu along the Umaji terraces before crates head downhill at first light.

STAGE 02 · SINGLE COLD PRESS

Pressed once beneath nitrogen seals

Whole fruit is gently halved and cold-pressed in spring water without heat pasteurization so volatile citrus brightness lands crisp in the can.
