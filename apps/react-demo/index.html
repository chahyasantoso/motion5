gerak examples

  • timing: letters drop in order, a dot hops in, a circle wipe covers the stage.
  • catch: a ball bounces to rest and a jointed arm reaches out and catches it.
  • splash: a drop falls into water and sends out rings and droplets.
  • card: a card that rises on hover and sinks while pressed.
  • pile: letters fall, tumble and squash as they pile up.
  • principles: a body crouches, leaps and lands, with follow-through on its antenna and word.
  • principles3d: the same leap in 3D, with small objects arriving and an orbiting camera.
