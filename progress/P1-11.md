## Invariant

Malformed composition and output-observation values are diagnosed at the runtime boundary and never published as ready values.

## Implementation

`Track` validates plugin and interpolator output as renderer-neutral immutable records. `GraphPublisher` validates composed output and output-observation sources before merge, emitting deterministic `composition-output-shape` and `observation-output-shape` diagnostics.

## Evidence

Red then green was real. The first implementation hit a typecheck error, diagnosed from the archived `ci-logs` branch, then a fixture asserted `observation-output-shape` through a source that correctly produced `composition-output-shape` and `blocked-upstream`. The fixture was corrected to seed an already-published malformed source and flush only the observer. Final CI passed quality, integration, boundaries, performance, and formatting.
