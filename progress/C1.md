## Invariant

The store's source subscription follows its listener set. The first listener attaches it, the last listener to leave releases it, and a later listener attaches it again and receives every subsequent patch. `getSnapshot()` never reports a patch older than the source while the store is detached.

## Failing-first evidence

Test: [`packages/react/test/patch-store-lifecycle.test.ts`](../packages/react/test/patch-store-lifecycle.test.ts).

On the parent commit `createPatchStore()` subscribed to the source once during construction and called that unsubscribe permanently when `listeners.size` reached zero. Both assertions below fail there:

1. after unsubscribe, resubscribe, and a new publish, the new listener records `[3]`; the parent records `[]`, and `getSnapshot()` still reports revision 1 rather than the revision 2 published while detached;
2. a StrictMode-style subscribe, teardown, subscribe records exactly one delivery; the parent records none.

The second assertion also guards the opposite failure: attaching per listener instead of per store would record the patch twice.

## Owner

`createPatchStore` owns the attach and release transition. `PatchRegistry` still owns patch identity, revisions, freezing, and delivery order; nothing in this slice touches the registry, the publisher, or the clock. The store adds no cache of its own beyond the snapshot the source already handed it.

## Motion5 boundary

Unchanged: the store stays framework-neutral and imports only `@motion5/core/internal` types, so `PatchRegistry` and graph internals stay out of the public package surface. No React dependency is introduced here; the hook and public exports are C2.

## Deletions

The construction-time subscription is removed. No compatibility shim replaces it.
