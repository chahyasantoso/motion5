## Invariant

A flush requested while patch subscribers are being notified never runs inside that flush: it is coalesced into exactly one follow-up invalidation that the next flush drains.

## Evidence

The failing-first branch proves the parent behavior and the implementation run proves the fix. Green CI covered quality, integration, and boundaries. The recovery audit contract artifact separately re-proves the rescue's GSAP, DOM, React, and boundary surfaces; it does not replace A3's focused evidence.

## Policy and ownership

`PatchRegistry` owns the notification window, `GraphPublisher` rejects traversal at the same boundary, and `GraphRuntime` queues one follow-up seed set for the next clock tick. No second clock or public export was added. `ProjectRuntime.seek()` inherits the policy through `GraphRuntime.invalidate()`.
